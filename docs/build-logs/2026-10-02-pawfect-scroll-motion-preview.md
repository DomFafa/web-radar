# Pawfect scroll-motion preview — 2026-10-02

## Scope

Add expressive bidirectional scroll motion to the accepted Everyday Objects site without changing its copy, assets, typography, colors, layout, material contract or production release. This is a local review candidate; it is not imported into the frozen Pawfect `.2` renderer.

The first preview's similar fade/slide entrances were rejected as monotonous. The revised `src/templates/themes/pawfect/motion.ts` gives each section a different sequence: the hero title flies from the left while the artwork flies from the lower right; the badge spins in later and the photo tag follows. Product cards unfold in a center/left/right fan, large scene panels open around a side hinge, gallery photographs wipe in from three directions, and the FAQ/closing headline settles back from an enlarged foreground. Hero entrances take 1.6–1.95 seconds; later main scenes take 1.9–2.4 seconds with visible staggered starts.

The third review revision (`expressive-v3`) addresses repeated page choreography and repeated entrances. Each element is consumed on its first visible entry within the current document, including when reduced motion or keyboard focus skips its animation. Scrolling back, resizing, restoring a cached page or toggling motion preferences never rearms it. A new document visit starts a new sequence. All scroll-driven drift and the original infinite portrait float are removed; content settles into its original position.

Page compositions now differ: home retains its large opposing flights and fan cards; catalog has a folding headline and rising shelf cards; about opens from the center with circular and diagonal photo reveals; contact uses a hanging title, folded form and sliding information panel. The three saved detail pages receive turntable, iris and descending-image compositions, each with a different headline sequence. The local replay script supplies the saved product order as a script data attribute; no renderer, material contract or production API is changed.

A later scroll review reproduced a flash of the final pose before the entrance: a heading was already visible at 94% of viewport height, while motion only started after crossing 88%. Pending targets now receive their hidden opacity during setup, and restore their original inline opacity in the same task that creates the entrance. Focus and reduced-motion handling also restore pending content. A small head bootstrap covers the external script's first-load gap; when motion fails to load within 1.5 seconds it reveals the static page and skips a late animation start. JavaScript-disabled and reduced-motion views remain readable directly.

Native scrolling, links and horizontal galleries remain available. Temporary horizontal clipping contains off-canvas flight paths without adding a scroll container. Reduced-motion preferences cancel effects and restore overflow settings. Gallery frames and captions stay stationary while only the photographs reveal. No new image is generated or substituted.
