Frequently asked questions

Reduce everyday stress through mindful breathing exercises that encourage peace and mental clarity.

01.
What should I bring to my first class?

Please bring a yoga mat, a small hand towel, and a water bottle, and wear comfortable, breathable clothing you can move freely in.

02.
Which yoga class is right for me?

Beginners often start with our Vinyasa or Outdoor Yoga sessions, while Power Yoga is generally better suited for more experienced practitioners.

03.
Do I need to be flexible before starting yoga?

Not at all — flexibility isn't a requirement to begin, it naturally develops over time the more consistently you attend and practice yoga.

04.
How long is each yoga class?

Most of our sessions run between 60 and 75 minutes in total, which includes a short warm-up period at the start and a calming cool-down.

05.
Can I cancel or reschedule my booking?

Yes, bookings can be cancelled or rescheduled up to 24 hours in advance, directly through your account dashboard or by contacting us.
